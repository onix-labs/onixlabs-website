Matthew is a highly experienced software engineer and technical leader with a strong focus on building pragmatic, well-architected solutions that empower developers and elevate the craft of software engineering. With over 15 years in the industry, he has specialised in designing elegant, maintainable systems across enterprise domains, consistently championing clean architecture, developer experience, and thoughtful tooling.

As the Founding Engineer at ONIXLabs, Matthew leads an engineering-driven company dedicated to building open-source and licensed developer tools, frameworks, and libraries, primarily within the .NET, Kotlin, and Corda ecosystems. His work at ONIXLabs reflects a passion for creating modular, expressive, and production-ready software infrastructure that enhances developer productivity and clarity.

Previously, Matthew served as Principal Software Engineer at R3, where he played a key role in shaping the Corda platform and led strategic efforts across advanced ledger design and digital infrastructure for regulated financial systems. His contributions culminated in the delivery of a prototype orchestration network for central bank digital currencies.

He has also held technical leadership positions at Ivno and TradeIX, where he was instrumental in architecting enterprise-grade platforms used across the fintech and trade finance sectors.

A lifelong programmer, Matthew is deeply interested in language design, type systems, and developer ergonomics. He contributes regularly to open-source and enjoys creating tools that make software development more expressive, maintainable, and robust. As a top 1% Stack Overflow contributor, his insights have reached over six million developers worldwide. His approach to engineering blends precision, craftsmanship, and a relentless focus on improving the tools developers depend on every day.

Outside of work, Matthew continues to explore technology hands-on, whether building custom PCs, restoring retro computers from the 1990s and early 2000s, or prototyping with Lego Technic. A lifelong enthusiast of steam locomotives, he dreams of constructing a detailed model railway that captures the twilight of the steam era in South Africa, circa 1975–1995.
