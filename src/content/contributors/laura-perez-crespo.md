Laura is a skilled software engineer specializing in enterprise application development with a focus on Kotlin, Corda, and TypeScript. A graduate of the Escuela de Ingeniería de Telecomunicación at the Universidad de Vigo, Laura has delivered high-impact contributions across complex distributed systems in the financial technology space.

Prior to her contributions at ONIXLabs, Laura played a pivotal role in the development of TradeIX’s Marco Polo Platform and Ivno’s Token Collateralization Platform, where she was responsible for core server-side functionality, deployment infrastructure, and system security.

At ONIXLabs, Laura brings her sharp technical acumen and exceptional attention to detail to the team’s open-source initiatives. She ensures every component is not only production-ready but rigorously tested, well-documented, and peer-reviewed—contributing significantly to the quality and reliability of the ONIXLabs ecosystem.

Bilingual in Spanish and English, Laura has an uncanny ability to spot English grammar and spelling mistakes that even native speakers overlook. Her precision in both code and communication helps uphold a high standard across the board.

Outside of work, Laura enjoys horseback riding and has a keen eye for fashion, bringing the same elegance and care to her hobbies as she does to her engineering craft.
