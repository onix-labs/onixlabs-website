This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use this website or contact us, why we collect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have. We keep it as short as we can while giving you everything the law requires.

## Who we are

This website is operated by **ONIXLABS LIMITED**, trading as ONIXLabs, a private limited company registered in England and Wales with company number 13661968. Our registered office is 27 Old Gloucester Street, London, WC1N 3AX, United Kingdom.

For the personal data described in this policy, we are the **controller**: we decide how and why it's used. We're a small company and haven't appointed a data protection officer, as we're not required to. For anything about your personal data, email [admin@onixlabs.io](mailto:admin@onixlabs.io).

This policy doesn't cover personal data we handle on behalf of our clients during a project. In that case the client is the controller, and our contract with them sets out how we handle it.

## The short version

- The website sets **no cookies** and runs **no analytics or tracking**.
- We only receive personal data that you choose to send us: through the contact form, by email, or by booking a meeting.
- We use it to reply to you and, if we end up working together, to run that relationship.
- We never sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

## What we collect and why

### When you use the contact form

The form asks for your **name**, **email address**, **reason for getting in touch** and **message**. We use these to read and reply to your enquiry.

- **Lawful basis:** our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. Where your enquiry is about working with us, it's also processing needed to take steps, at your request, before entering into a contract.
- **How it reaches us:** the form is processed by Formspree, which forwards your submission to our email inbox.

### When you email us

We receive your email address, name and whatever you include in your message, and we use them to reply. The lawful bases are the same as for the contact form.

### When you book a meeting

The "Book a meeting" links take you to our Calendly booking page. If you book, Calendly passes us your **name**, **email address**, the **time** you chose and any **notes** you add, which we use to hold the meeting. The lawful basis is the same as for the contact form. Calendly's own privacy notice also applies to your use of its site.

### When you visit the website

The website is static and hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like any web host, GitHub records technical information about each request, including your **IP address**, browser and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't receive these logs, and we don't use them to identify or profile visitors. See [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for how GitHub handles them.

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice for the rest of your visit. That stays on your device; we never receive it. Our [Cookie Policy](/Legal/Cookies) explains this in more detail.

### When we work together

If an enquiry becomes a business relationship, we keep the contact details and correspondence needed to deliver our services, invoice and keep proper records.

- **Lawful basis:** performing our contract with you or your organisation, our legitimate interest in managing business relationships, and our legal obligations, such as keeping accounting records.

## Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, who act on our instructions:

| Provider | What they do | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Formspree | Processes contact form submissions and forwards them to us | United States |
| Namecheap (Private Email) | Hosts our business email inbox | United States |
| Calendly | Handles meeting bookings | United States |
| GitHub | Hosts this website | United States |

We may also share personal data when the law requires it, to establish or defend legal claims, or with professional advisers, such as accountants or lawyers, who are bound by confidentiality. We don't sell personal data or share it for marketing.

## International transfers

The providers above are based in, or process data in, the United States. When personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on a lawful transfer mechanism: UK adequacy regulations, including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, which contractually require the provider to protect your data to UK standards. You can ask us for more detail about the safeguards for a particular provider.

## How long we keep it

- **Enquiries** that don't lead to working together: deleted two years after our last contact, from both our inbox and the contact form service.
- **Meeting bookings:** kept with the related enquiry and deleted on the same schedule.
- **Client relationships:** kept for as long as the relationship lasts, then for as long as we need to meet legal obligations. For example, financial records are kept for six years after the end of the financial year they relate to.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you
- have inaccurate data **corrected**
- have your data **erased**, where there's no good reason for us to keep it
- **restrict** how we use your data
- **object** to our using it on the basis of legitimate interests
- **data portability**, in some circumstances

To use any of these rights, email [admin@onixlabs.io](mailto:admin@onixlabs.io). We'll respond within one month, and there's normally no charge. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

We don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your personal data, please tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.

## Children

This website is aimed at businesses and software professionals, not children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when our practices change, and the date at the top of the page shows when it last changed. If a change significantly affects how we use personal data we already hold, we'll tell the people affected directly.
