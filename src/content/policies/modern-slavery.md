## A voluntary statement

Section 54 of the **Modern Slavery Act 2015** requires commercial organizations with an annual turnover of £36 million or more to publish a slavery and human trafficking statement. ONIXLABS LIMITED is well below that threshold, so this statement is **voluntary**, and it isn't a statement made under that section. We publish it because we oppose modern slavery and human trafficking, and because our clients may reasonably want to know our position.

## Our business

ONIXLABS LIMITED is a software engineering company registered in England and Wales. We develop software products and open-source libraries, and provide software engineering and consulting services to businesses. The company is run by its director, who does most of the work, sometimes with contractors and associates.

## Our supply chain

Our supply chain is small and consists mainly of:

- software, cloud and online services from established technology providers, such as hosting, email, source control and productivity tools
- computer equipment bought from mainstream suppliers
- professional services, such as accounting
- independent contractors and associates engaged for specific work

## Risk

Because our work is professional and digital, and we work with established suppliers, we consider the risk of modern slavery in our own operations to be low. The greatest risk lies further along the supply chains of the hardware we buy, which we have little ability to influence directly.

## What we do

- We pay the people who work for us fairly and on time, and engage contractors on clear written terms.
- We prefer established suppliers that publish their own commitments on modern slavery and responsible sourcing.
- We expect our suppliers and contractors to comply with the Modern Slavery Act 2015, and we won't knowingly work with anyone who doesn't.
- We'll investigate any concern raised with us and act on what we find, including ending a relationship where appropriate.

## Reporting a concern

If you have a concern about modern slavery connected with ONIXLabs, please tell us at [admin@onixlabs.io](mailto:admin@onixlabs.io). If you believe someone is in immediate danger, call 999. You can also contact the Modern Slavery and Exploitation Helpline on 08000 121 700.
