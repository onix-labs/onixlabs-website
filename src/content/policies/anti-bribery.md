ONIXLABS LIMITED does not tolerate bribery or corruption in any form. We win and keep work on the quality of what we do, and on nothing else. This policy explains how we put that into practice and meet our obligations under the **Bribery Act 2010**.

## Who it applies to

This policy applies to ONIXLABS LIMITED and its director, and to anyone who performs services for us or on our behalf, including contractors, associates and agents. Matthew Layton, as director, is responsible for it.

## What we don't do

We never:

- offer, promise or give anything of value to anyone, directly or through someone else, to improperly influence a decision or to reward improper conduct
- request, agree to receive or accept anything of value in return for acting improperly
- offer or give anything of value to a public official, in the UK or elsewhere, to influence them in their official role
- make **facilitation payments**, which are small unofficial payments to speed up a routine government action, even where they're customary
- use contractors, agents or other third parties to do any of these things on our behalf

## Gifts and hospitality

Modest gifts and hospitality can be a normal part of building working relationships. We only give or accept them when they're:

- reasonable and proportionate in value, and not cash or a cash equivalent
- given openly, not in secret
- not intended, and not likely to be seen as intended, to influence a business decision
- not given or received during a tender, contract negotiation or other decision about a client's work with us

We keep a record of any gift or hospitality given or received with a value of more than £100.

## Conflicts of interest

We avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any potential conflict to the client concerned, as described in our [Code of Conduct and Ethics](/Legal/Code-Of-Conduct).

## Working with others

We choose contractors and suppliers on merit, and we expect anyone who works on our behalf to comply with this policy and with the Bribery Act 2010.

## Reporting a concern

If you're asked to make or accept a bribe, or you suspect bribery or corruption connected with ONIXLabs, please tell us at [admin@onixlabs.io](mailto:admin@onixlabs.io). We'll treat what you tell us confidentially and act on it. Nobody who raises a concern in good faith will be treated unfavorably for doing so.

## Review

We review this policy at least once a year, and whenever our business changes in a way that affects it.
