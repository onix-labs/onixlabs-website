ONIXLabs is committed to treating everyone fairly, and to building software and working relationships that include everyone. Good engineering depends on diverse perspectives, and we want everyone we work with to feel respected and able to contribute.

## Our commitment

We don't discriminate against anyone because of any of the characteristics protected by the **Equality Act 2010**: age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex, and sexual orientation. Nor do we treat people unfairly for any other reason unrelated to their work.

## How we put it into practice

- **Working with people.** When we engage contractors or collaborators, we choose them on their skills, experience and the needs of the work, and treat them fairly and consistently.
- **Our clients.** We treat every client and prospective client with the same respect and professionalism.
- **Respectful conduct.** We don't tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination, in any setting, as our [Code of Conduct and Ethics](/Legal/Code-Of-Conduct) sets out.
- **Accessible work.** We aim to make our website, documentation and software usable by as many people as possible. Our [Accessibility Statement](/Legal/Accessibility) explains our approach to the website.
- **Open source.** We welcome contributions to our open-source projects from anyone, and we judge contributions on their merits.
- **Reasonable adjustments.** If you need us to work differently to take part fully, for example in how we communicate or meet, just tell us.

## Who is responsible

ONIXLabs is a small company, and its director, Matthew Layton, is responsible for this commitment and for reviewing it at least once a year.

## Raising a concern

If you feel you've been treated unfairly by ONIXLabs, please tell us at [admin@onixlabs.io](mailto:admin@onixlabs.io). We'll take it seriously, look into it promptly and confidentially, and tell you what we've done.
