This code sets out how ONIXLabs conducts its business. It's short and practical on purpose: it describes what our clients, collaborators and the wider community can expect from us, and what we expect in return.

## Who it applies to

The code applies to ONIXLABS LIMITED and its director, and to anyone who works with us or on our behalf, including contractors and associates. Matthew Layton, as director, is responsible for it and reviews it at least once a year.

## Honesty and integrity

- We describe our products, services and experience accurately, and we don't claim certifications, results or capabilities we don't have.
- We give honest estimates and tell clients promptly when something changes, including when the news is unwelcome.
- We keep accurate business and financial records.
- We comply with the law wherever we work, including the Bribery Act 2010, as described in our [Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy](/Legal/Anti-Bribery).

## Quality and professional judgment

- We only take on work we're competent to deliver, and we say so when something is outside our expertise.
- We treat code as a craft: we review it, test it, and take responsibility for what we ship.
- Where we use AI tools in our work, a person reviews the result and remains accountable for it, and we use them only in ways that respect each client's agreement and data protection obligations.

## Confidentiality and data

- We keep our clients' confidential information confidential, and use it only for the work it was shared for.
- We handle personal data lawfully and securely, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/Legal/Privacy) and [Information Security](/Legal/Information-Security) overview.

## Conflicts of interest

We avoid situations where our personal or commercial interests could conflict with a client's, or could appear to. If a potential conflict arises, we disclose it to the client promptly and agree how to handle it, which may mean declining the work.

## Respectful conduct

We treat everyone we work with fairly and with respect. We don't tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination of any kind, in person or online, including in the open-source communities we take part in. Our commitment to fair treatment is set out in our [Equality, Diversity and Inclusion](/Legal/Equality-Diversity-Inclusion) statement.

## Open source and intellectual property

We respect intellectual property. We follow the licenses of the open-source software we use and contribute to, we credit others' work, and we're clear with clients about the third-party components in what we deliver.

## Raising a concern

If you think we haven't lived up to this code, please tell us at [admin@onixlabs.io](mailto:admin@onixlabs.io). We'll treat what you tell us seriously and confidentially, look into it, and tell you what we've done. Nobody who raises a concern in good faith will be treated unfavorably for doing so.
