Cookies are small files that websites store on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local and session storage, work the same way. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), websites must tell you what they store and ask for your consent, unless what they store is strictly necessary for something you've asked for.

## What this website stores

This website sets **no cookies**, and it doesn't use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. It loads nothing from other websites while you browse it.

It stores one thing, and only if you use the light and dark mode switch:

| Name | Type | Purpose | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theme-preference` | Session storage | Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode as you move between pages | Until you close the tab |

It's stored only because you asked for it, by using the switch, so it's exempt from the consent requirement. It never leaves your device, and we never receive it.

## Why there's no cookie banner

Because the website stores nothing that needs your consent, there's nothing for a banner or a preferences control to manage. If we ever add anything that needs consent, such as analytics, we'll ask for it before storing anything, and give you a way to change your mind at any time.

## Other websites

Some links on this site take you to other services, such as Calendly to book a meeting, GitHub to download our software, or our social media profiles. Those sites may set their own cookies under their own policies, which we don't control.

## Managing storage in your browser

You can clear session storage, and block or delete cookies, in your browser's settings. Clearing it here simply returns the site to following your device's light or dark setting.
